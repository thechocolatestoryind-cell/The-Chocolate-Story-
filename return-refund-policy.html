Returns

Because our products are food items, gifts and made-to-order treats, we do not offer routine returns, for example for a change of mind. Please check the product description, options, delivery information and your delivery address carefully before you order.

Refunds

We do not offer routine refunds. This does not stop us from looking into a genuine problem with your order. If something is wrong with what you receive, please report it as described below, and we will review it.

Reporting a wrong or damaged product

If you receive a wrong product or a damaged product, please contact us so that we can review the issue.

What to send us

  • Your order number (shown on your order page).
  • Clear photos of the product received and, where relevant, of the packaging and any label.
  • A short description of what is wrong.
  • The name and phone number used for the order.

Please contact us as soon as you can after the delivery, because it helps us to review the matter properly. We do not set a fixed reporting deadline in this policy.

How to contact us

Email thechocolatestory.ind@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp (+91 88470 50895). Please never send passwords, OTPs, card numbers, CVVs or PINs.

How we review your report

  1. We check your order and the information and photos you send.
  2. We review the issue and may ask you for further details.
  3. We tell you what resolution, if any, is available for your case.

Reporting a problem does not automatically entitle you to a refund, replacement or store credit. Any remedy is offered only after we have reviewed the issue and told you what we can do. We do not promise approval or fixed processing times in advance.

If a refund is approved

If we approve a refund in a particular case, we will tell you how it will be made. Money is considered refunded only after it has actually been authorised and processed; we will not tell you it has been refunded before that. Timing can also depend on your bank or payment provider.

Relationship with cancellations

Cancelling an order before it ships is covered by our Cancellation Policy and is separate from this policy. Once an order is marked Shipped, it cannot be cancelled.

Your legal rights

This policy is intended to be fair and transparent. It does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws.
