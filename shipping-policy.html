Delivery coverage

We advertise delivery across India. However, delivery availability can differ from product to product. Some products – for example freshly made or perishable items – may be available for local delivery only, and some locations may not be serviceable for some products.

Where a product is subject to a restriction, this should be indicated on the product page or during checkout. Please check this before ordering, and Contact page us if you are unsure whether we can deliver a particular product to your location. We do not promise that every product can be delivered to every location.

Estimated delivery time

Our current estimate is around 6–8 days from the order date. This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Actual delivery times can vary with the product, your location, courier operations, weather, festivals and other circumstances outside our control.

If a product page or checkout shows different delivery information for a specific product, that product-specific information applies.

Shipping charges

Delivery charges are set per product. They are shown on the product page and are included in the order summary in your cart and at checkout, so you can see the total before you pay. Charges can differ from one product to another. We do not add hidden delivery fees after payment.

Order processing and dispatch

After you place an order, it moves through the statuses shown on your order page: Pending, Confirmed, Being prepared, Shipped and Delivered, or Cancelled. An order is treated as paid and confirmed only when the order page shows it as such.

Some products are prepared after the order is received, so processing time is part of the estimated delivery time above. Cancellation is possible only until an order is marked Shipped; see the Cancellation Policy.

Tracking

When we add shipment information to your order, your order details page can show the courier name, a tracking number and, where available, a tracking link. Shipment information may not be available for every order or at every stage, and we do not promise live tracking or automatic delivery notifications. If you need an update, contact us with your order number.

Delivery address and availability of the receiver

  • Please make sure your delivery address, PIN code and phone number are complete and correct before you pay.
  • If you need to correct an address, contact us as early as possible. A change may not be possible once an order has been shipped.
  • Please make sure someone is available to receive the order. Where a product has storage or handling guidance, follow it after delivery.

Delayed or failed delivery

If your order has not arrived within the estimated time, contact us with your order number and we will look into it. If a delivery cannot be completed – for example because of an incorrect or incomplete address, or because the receiver is unavailable – please contact us promptly so that we can review the options with you. We do not state any additional delivery fees in this policy.

Damaged or wrong item on arrival

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product, please follow the steps in our Return & Refund Policy and contact us with your order number and photos.
