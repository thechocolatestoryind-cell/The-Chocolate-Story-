How we can help

Product questions

Ask about ingredients, allergies, options, personalisation, gifting ideas or custom requests. Please mention the product name or share its link from the Shop.

Order enquiries

Include your order number, which is shown on your order page under My orders, along with the name and phone number used for the order.

Delivery issues

Tell us your order number, the delivery PIN code and what has happened. Please also read our Shipping Policy.

Wrong or damaged products

Send your order number, clear photos of the product and its packaging, and a short description of the problem. Read the Return & Refund Policy for what to expect.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled only before they are marked Shipped. Contact us as early as possible with your order number. See the Cancellation Policy.

Please keep your payment details private

Never send passwords, OTPs, card numbers, CVVs or PINs to us by email, WhatsApp, Instagram or any other message. We will never ask you for them.
