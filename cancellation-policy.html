When an order can be cancelled

An order can be cancelled only before its status changes to Shipped. Once an order is marked Shipped, cancellation is not available under our policy.

You can see your order’s current status on your order details page. Orders move through Pending, Confirmed, Being prepared, Shipped and Delivered. Please act quickly if you need to cancel, because an order can be marked Shipped at any time.

How to request a cancellation

  1. Contact us promptly on WhatsApp (+91 88470 50895) or by email at thechocolatestory.ind@gmail.com. If your order page shows a cancellation request option for your order, you may use it as well.
  2. Include your order number and the name and phone number used for the order.
  3. We will check the order’s status.
  4. We will tell you whether the order can be cancelled.

Sending a message or a request does not automatically cancel the order or guarantee approval. An order is cancelled only when we confirm it, or when your order page shows it as Cancelled.

If your order has already shipped

Once an order is Shipped, it cannot be cancelled. If there is a genuine problem with the product you receive, such as a wrong or damaged item, please read our Return & Refund Policy and contact us with your order number and relevant photos.

Cancellation is not the same as a refund

Cancelling an order and receiving money back are separate steps. A cancellation request is about stopping the order before it ships. If a payment has been made, what happens to it is decided and processed separately, as described in our Return & Refund Policy.

We will not say that money has been refunded until a refund has actually been authorised and processed. We do not state cancellation fees or processing times in this policy.

Cancellations by us

We may need to cancel or decline an order – for example if a product is unavailable, an order cannot be delivered to the address given, or there is a clear pricing or listing error. If this happens, we will contact you and explain the next steps.
