Who we are

The Chocolate Story brings together chocolates, cakes, desserts, dessert bowls, gifts and hampers in one warm, chocolate-inspired shop. Our aim is simple: to help you find something delicious for a celebration, a thank-you, or just a sweet moment.

We advertise our range as 100% eggless. If you have an allergy or a specific dietary requirement, please read the product description and message us before ordering so that we can answer your question properly.

What you will find in our shop

Our Shop page shows the live catalogue, organised by category. Depending on what is currently listed, you can browse:

  • Chocolates and gifts
  • Cakes
  • Desserts and dessert bowls
  • Hampers, when listed

Products, photos, prices and availability are managed by us and can change as items are added, updated or become unavailable. What you see on the Shop page is always the most current information.

How we like to work

  • Care for the details. From the way a product is described to the way it is presented, we want the experience to feel thoughtful.
  • Clear information. Prices and delivery charges are shown on the product page, in your cart and at checkout before you pay.
  • Helpful, respectful service. We are happy to answer questions before and after you order.

How ordering works

  1. Browse the Shop by category, or use the search box.
  2. Open a product to see its photos, description, price and delivery charge.
  3. If the product offers options – for example a size, a flavour or a personalised message – choose them. The price updates to reflect your selection.
  4. Add the product to your cart and review the items and totals.
  5. Sign in with Google to check out, then choose or add a delivery address.
  6. Pay online. Your order then appears under My orders, where you can follow its status.

Online payment is the way to pay on our website; Cash on Delivery is not offered.

Custom and personalised requests

If you are looking for something that is not listed – for a special occasion, or a personalised design – use the Custom order button on our home page to message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, or contact us directly. The details, availability and pricing of custom requests are discussed with us.

Delivery

We deliver across India, but delivery availability can differ from product to product, and some products may be available only for local delivery. Please check the product page and your checkout, and contact us if you are unsure.

Our current estimate is around 6–8 days from the order date. This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Read the full Shipping Policy for details.
