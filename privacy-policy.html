Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how The Chocolate Story (“we”, “us”) handles personal information when you use this website. It applies to browsing the shop, signing in, saving addresses and wishlists, placing orders and contacting us through the website or the contact details published on it.

It describes how the website works at the time it was last updated (see the date at the top). Some features, such as online payment, may be switched on only at a later date; where that is the case, this policy says “if” or “when”.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. You can add an optional phone number to your profile.
  • Delivery addresses: receiver name, phone number, address lines, landmark, city, state, PIN code and country.
  • Order information: when ordering is available, the products, quantities and options you choose (including any personalised text you type), the delivery address, an optional order note, and the order’s totals and status.
  • Wishlist: the products you save.
  • Reviews: your rating and review text, where you submit a review.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, WhatsApp or Instagram, such as questions, custom-order details and photos of a problem.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and technology providers may receive technical information when your browser loads the site, such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages requested. We do not currently load analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

Information we do not collect

This website has no fields for card numbers, CVVs, card PINs, UPI PINs, bank passwords or OTPs, and we do not ask for them. Please do not send such details to us by email, WhatsApp, Instagram or any other message.

Google sign-in and your account

Customer features such as checkout, saved addresses, wishlist and order history require signing in with Google (through Firebase Authentication). We do not see or store your Google password. When you sign in for the first time we create a profile for you that holds your name, email address, profile photo link and optional phone number. You can edit your name and phone number on your profile page.

Cart, saved addresses, wishlist and order history

  • Cart: your cart is kept in your browser’s local storage on your own device (product, selected options, quantity and the price information shown when you added it). It is not saved to your account, and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Saved addresses and default address: stored in your account so that you can reuse them at checkout. You can add, edit and delete them.
  • Wishlist: stored in your account and linked to your account identifier.
  • Orders and shipments: where ordering is available, order records, shipment details and payment status are linked to your account so that you can see them on your order pages.

Reviews, custom-order requests and support messages

Reviews you submit are linked to your account and are shown publicly only after they have been approved. Custom-order requests and support messages are sent through WhatsApp, Instagram or email; those messages are handled on those services and in our own inboxes, and are also subject to the privacy practices of those services.

How we use information

  • To provide the website, keep you signed in, and remember your cart, addresses and wishlist.
  • To process, prepare, deliver and support orders, and to show you your order status.
  • To contact you about an order, a question or a problem you have reported.
  • To show approved reviews, and to keep the website working and secure.
  • To meet legal, accounting and dispute-resolution needs where required.

We use your information for these purposes and not to build advertising profiles.

Service providers and third parties

We use the following services. Each handles information under its own terms and privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in, and storage of profiles, addresses, wishlists, reviews and order records.
  • Website hosting: the site’s files are served by our hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages), which may log technical request information.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google’s servers, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • Cloudinary: product, banner and category images are delivered from Cloudinary’s servers.
  • Razorpay (online payment): our checkout is designed to use Razorpay as the payment provider. If and when online payment is offered, you enter payment details in Razorpay’s secure payment window; they are handled by Razorpay and not entered on or stored by this website. We receive the payment status and reference information needed to confirm your order.
  • Couriers and delivery partners: to deliver your order we share the receiver’s name, address and phone number with the delivery partner used for your order.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and email providers: used when you contact us or follow our links.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it or to protect our rights in a dispute.

Browser storage and similar technologies

This website uses your browser’s storage to work: your cart (local storage), your sign-in session (managed by Firebase Authentication), a temporary checkout reference during payment (session storage) and cached copies of site files so that pages load faster and some pages work offline (service worker cache). These are not used for advertising.

Storage, access and security

Information in your account is stored with Google Firebase. Access rules are designed so that customers can read and change only their own profile, addresses, wishlist and order information, and authorised staff access order information to run the shop. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We keep information for as long as it is needed for the purposes in this policy, including supporting orders and resolving disputes, and for as long as the law requires. We do not publish fixed retention periods.

Your choices and requests

  • Edit your name and phone number on your profile page, and manage your saved addresses and wishlist from your account.
  • Clear your cart by removing items or clearing your browser data.
  • Ask us to correct information, or to delete your account data, by emailing thechocolatestory.ind@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp (+91 88470 50895). We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

When you ask us to delete data, we will delete what we can. We may need to keep certain records, such as order and payment records, where they are needed to complete or support an order, to resolve a dispute, or to meet legal or accounting obligations. Deleting your data from our systems does not delete messages held by WhatsApp, Instagram, Google or other independent services.

Children

This website is meant for adults placing orders, or for young people with a parent or guardian’s involvement. We do not knowingly collect information from children for their own accounts.

External links

The website links to other services, such as WhatsApp and Instagram. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or the services we use change, for example when online payment is switched on. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
